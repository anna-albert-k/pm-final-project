BU Pay & Credit Roadmap

Riverty's first consumer card · Module 4 prioritization

Team (assumed): 4 engineers, 1 designer, 1 PM Window: 12 weeks, 2 week sprints Sample data, fictional
Strategic anchors
  • Persona: Heavy BNPL user aged 25-34, 4+ pay later orders a month across several providers, who wants to pay only for what she keeps and see what she owes in one place.
  • Primary metric: Month 2 activity rate of activated heavy users. Baseline 51.0% to 57.1%, target 65% or more.
  • Moment of misery: She cannot see in one place what she owes on the card and on regular pay later orders (N-07, T-103), and gets two reminders for different amounts in the same week (T-104).
  • Guardrail: Existing pay later checkout volume per active user must not fall, and overall decline rate stays at or below 3.5%.
